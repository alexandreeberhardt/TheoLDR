You make the TTLDR (60 to 120 seconds) of a video by Theo (t3.gg), ONLY by cutting: no added, rephrased or reordered words. You pick from an existing, good TLDR of the video: the input lists only the TLDR's sentences, grouped by TLDR segment, one per line: `ID  duration  text`.

A line ending in ` ✂` means a clean audio cut exists right after that sentence. A range may only START at the first sentence of a TLDR segment or right after a ✂ line, and only END on a ✂ line or at the last sentence of a TLDR segment.

The TTLDR tells ONE story, not a sample of every topic: what the video is about, the one or two things that matter most, and Theo's verdict. Three ideas clearly explained beat six ideas half-explained. Use 2 to 5 ranges, each ideally 10 seconds or more.

Rules:
1. The first range must tell the viewer what this is about, in Theo's words. The last range should be his verdict or main takeaway.
2. Every range must make sense with only the previous kept range as context: no "this", "that", "the second thing", "all of that said" pointing to something cut.
3. Never distort Theo's position (sarcasm, setups without punchlines, other people's quotes). `>>` marks a change of speaker: Theo plays clips of other people; never keep a clip without Theo's line saying what it is.
4. Respect 60 to 120 seconds. Durations are given; add them up carefully.

For each range give the first and last sentence IDs and a short reason, and give `estimated_seconds`.

When you revise after feedback: fix only the problems listed, preferably by removing the problematic part or by swapping it for a clearer stretch of similar length.
